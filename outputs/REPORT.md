# Executive Summary — E-Commerce Performance Analysis (2023–2024)

## Data quality actions
- Removed **185** duplicate transaction lines; standardised region labels to **4** clean values
- Fixed **20** impossible ages and imputed **178** missing ages (median)
- Net revenue is defined as **delivered orders only** (cancelled and returned orders excluded)

## Headline KPIs
- Net revenue: **1,509,681.2**
- Gross profit: **514,943.2**
- Gross margin %: **34.1**
- Delivered orders: **9,670.0**
- Active customers: **3,854.0**
- Avg order value: **156.1**
- Repeat purchase rate %: **53.8**
- Cancel rate %: **3.0**
- Return rate %: **4.9**

## Key findings
1. **Seasonality:** peak month was **December 2024**; revenue grew **108% YoY** (2024 vs 2023).
2. **Category mix:** `Home & Kitchen` leads on revenue, but `Beauty` has the best margin (58%) — `Electronics` the worst (19%).
3. **Concentration:** top 10 products = **72%** of revenue; **14** of 60 products generate 80%.
4. **Discounting:** margin falls from **37%** (no discount) to **21%** at 20% off.
5. **RFM:** Champions are **21%** of customers but **40%** of revenue; **794** high-value customers are At Risk (win-back target).
6. **Retention:** average cohort retention is **20%** at month 1, **16%** at month 3, **13%** at month 6.
7. **Channel ROI:** best LTV:CAC = `Organic` (8.5x), worst = `Paid Search` (2.1x).
8. **Forecast:** next 6 months projected at **$684,323** (rolling backtest MAPE **20.4%**; treat as directional).

## Recommendations
- Shift acquisition budget from `Paid Search` toward `Organic` and other channels above the 3x LTV:CAC benchmark.
- Launch a win-back campaign for the At Risk segment before the Nov–Dec peak; cap blanket 20% discounts which erode margin.
- Build a month-1 onboarding flow to lift early retention, the steepest drop-off point.
- Stock up on top-revenue SKUs ahead of Q4 using the forecast.
